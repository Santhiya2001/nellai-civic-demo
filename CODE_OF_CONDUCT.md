# Community code of conduct

Be respectful, patient and constructive. Welcome people with different experience levels and backgrounds. Discuss ideas and code without attacking contributors. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, publish personal information or upload harmful/illegal content.

Maintainers may remove abusive content or restrict participation to protect the community. Report concerns privately to the repository maintainer; a public deployment should publish a monitored contact channel. Handle reports confidentially and fairly.
